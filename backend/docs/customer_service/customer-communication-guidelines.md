# Customer Communication Guidelines

## Tone
Calm, direct, and specific. Acknowledge the inconvenience once, briefly, then give the concrete
facts — status, cause if known, estimated restoration time. Repeated apologizing reads as
uncertain rather than helpful.

## Section 4.2 - Restoration estimates
When communicating a restoration estimate to a customer, always include the caveat:
"Restoration times are estimates and may change as crews assess the situation." Never state an
estimated restoration time as a guarantee.

## What representatives may and may not say
- May say: current outage status, cause if confirmed, crew status, the published restoration
  estimate, and general safety guidance drawn from the approved Safety Procedures document.
- May not say: a speculated cause before it's confirmed in the outage system; a promise of
  compensation unless the customer's eligibility has actually been confirmed against Outage
  Credit Policies; any electrical safety instruction beyond what Safety Procedures covers.

## Multi-channel consistency
Whatever is told to a customer by phone must match what's published on the outage map. If a
representative notices a mismatch, escalate to Grid Operations rather than resolving it
independently.

## Empathy without overcommitting
"I can see there's an outage affecting your area, and crews are working on it" is accurate and
reassuring. "I promise it'll be fixed by 4pm" is not — only the currently published restoration
estimate may be quoted, with the standard caveat above.
