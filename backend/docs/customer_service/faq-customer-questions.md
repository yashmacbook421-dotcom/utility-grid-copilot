# Frequently Asked Customer Questions

## "Why is my power out?"
Check the outage system for the customer's service area first. If an active outage is on file,
share status, cause, crew status, and estimated restoration time per Customer Communication
Guidelines. If nothing is on file, advise the customer to check their own breaker panel before
assuming a utility-side outage, and log a new report if it persists.

## "When will my power come back?"
Only quote the restoration estimate currently published in the outage system, with the standard
caveat that it may change as crews assess the situation.

## "Is there an outage in my area?"
Check the outage system by the customer's service area, not just their exact address. A "none"
result means no outage is currently on file for that area.

## "How do I start or stop service?"
Refer to Service Start/Stop Procedures for the specific requirements — ID, proof of address,
and notice period.

## "Am I eligible for an outage credit?"
Refer to Outage Credit Policies. Eligibility depends on the confirmed outage duration from the
outage system, not the customer's own estimate of how long they were out.

## "How do I report a downed power line?"
This is always an emergency escalation — follow Downed Power Line Procedures immediately, do
not treat it as a routine outage report.

## "What are the current electricity rates?"
Refer to Rate Schedules for the customer's specific enrolled plan. Never quote a rate from
memory.

## "How do I apply for assistance programs?"
Refer to Customer Assistance Programs for the relevant program — rate assistance, medical
baseline, or a payment plan — based on the customer's situation.
