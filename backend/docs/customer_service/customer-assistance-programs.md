# Customer Assistance Programs

## Low-income rate assistance
Households at or below 200% of the federal poverty line can enroll in a reduced-rate program
providing a 20-35% discount on the energy portion of their bill, tiered by household income and
size. Enrollment requires income documentation and is renewed annually.

## Medical baseline / medical necessity
Customers who depend on electrically powered medical equipment can enroll to receive priority
restoration status during outages, and protection from disconnection for nonpayment while a
payment plan is being arranged. Requires a physician's certification.

## Payment plans
A customer with a past-due balance may request a payment plan spreading the balance over up to
12 months, provided they stay current on new charges. Representatives can offer this without
supervisor approval for balances under $500; larger balances require supervisor sign-off.

## Energy assistance referrals
For balances too large for a standard payment plan, or for customers not eligible for the
rate-assistance program, refer the customer to the state or federal Low Income Home Energy
Assistance Program (LIHEAP). Representatives should provide the referral but never promise a
specific award amount, which LIHEAP determines independently.
