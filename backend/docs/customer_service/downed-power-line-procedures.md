# Downed Power Line Procedures

## Immediate instructions to the customer
"Do not approach or touch the downed line. Keep people, pets, and vehicles at least 30 feet
away. If the line is on or near a vehicle, occupants should stay inside unless there's an
immediate danger like fire — in that case, exit by jumping clear with both feet together and
shuffle away without touching the vehicle and ground at the same time."

## Representative actions
1. Get the exact location (cross streets or address) and whether the line appears to be
   sparking, smoking, or in contact with anything — a vehicle, structure, or water.
2. Open an emergency case immediately. This is always an escalation, never a standard outage
   ticket.
3. Dispatch to the utility's emergency line and field crew, and notify local emergency services
   (911) if there is any immediate danger to people — contact, fire, or a blocked roadway.
4. Do not close the case or tell the customer the situation is resolved until a field crew
   confirms the line has been de-energized and secured.

## Never say
Never tell a customer a downed line is "probably not live," "safe to move away from the road
carefully," or give any instruction to interact with the line, even indirectly. Treat every
downed line as energized regardless of appearance, sound, or how long it's been down.
