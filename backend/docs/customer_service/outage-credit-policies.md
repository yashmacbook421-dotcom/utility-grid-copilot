# Outage Credit Policies

## Eligibility
A residential customer is eligible for an automatic outage credit when a sustained outage (see
Outage Response Procedures) affecting their service point lasts longer than 4 continuous hours,
and the outage was not caused by a declared major weather event or a scheduled maintenance the
customer was notified of in advance.

## Credit amount
$25 credit for outages between 4 and 12 hours; $50 credit for outages exceeding 12 hours.
Additional credit for perishable-food loss requires a separate claim process and is not part of
this automatic credit.

## What representatives should do
Confirm the outage duration from the outage system before promising a credit. If the customer's
stated outage duration doesn't match the system record, do not promise a credit — advise the
customer that a review will be conducted and they'll be notified of the outcome within 5
business days.

## Exclusions
Outages caused by the customer's own equipment, declared major weather events (including Public
Safety Power Shutoffs), or outages where the account was already in a disconnect-for-nonpayment
state are not eligible for an automatic credit.
