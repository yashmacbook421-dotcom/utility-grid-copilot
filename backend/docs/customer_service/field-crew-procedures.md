# Field Crew Procedures

## Dispatch priority
Crews are dispatched in this order: (1) confirmed safety hazards such as downed lines or fire
risk, (2) major outages affecting more than 500 customers, (3) sustained outages ranked by
customer count, (4) scheduled or routine work.

## Crew status definitions
"Dispatched" means a crew is assigned and en route. "On site" means the crew is physically at
the location assessing or repairing. "Assessing damage" means the crew is on site but the cause
or repair time isn't yet determined — no restoration estimate should be treated as final until
this stage completes. "Completed" means the repair is finished and restoration is confirmed.

## Communication back to customer service
Field crews update the outage system's status field in real time. Customer service must always
pull the current system status rather than relying on a status relayed verbally or from a prior
shift, since crew status can change within minutes.

## Multi-crew events
For major events involving multiple crews, the outage system aggregates status at the
service-area level. A representative should not infer individual-customer-level detail beyond
what the aggregated area record shows.
