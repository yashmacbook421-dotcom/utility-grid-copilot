# Metering Procedures

## Standard meter reads
Most accounts use automated metering infrastructure (AMI) providing actual, not estimated,
daily reads. A small number of legacy accounts still require a physical meter read; those bills
may show "estimated" for a cycle if a read was missed, followed by a true-up adjustment on the
next actual read.

## Customer-reported meter issues
A customer reporting their meter "spinning fast," making noise, sparking, or showing visible
damage or scorching must be treated as a safety report — escalate per Safety Procedures rather
than scheduling a routine meter service visit.

## Disputed reads
A customer disputing a specific read may request a meter accuracy test. Representatives should
note the disputed read and usage in the case and inform the customer that a field test
typically takes 5-10 business days. Billing for the disputed period is not automatically paused
pending the test unless the customer requests a payment plan.

## Smart meter opt-out
Customers may opt out of AMI in favor of a manually read analog meter, which may incur a
monthly opt-out fee and results in estimated, not real-time, usage data between manual reads.
