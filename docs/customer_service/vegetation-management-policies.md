# Vegetation Management Policies

## Purpose
Tree and brush contact with power lines is a leading cause of both outages and wildfire
ignition risk. Vegetation management — tree trimming and line clearance near utility
infrastructure — is a preventive program, distinct from outage response.

## Clearance standards
A minimum clearance of 4 feet is maintained around distribution lines and 10 feet around
transmission lines during routine trimming cycles; higher clearance may be required in
designated high-fire-risk areas.

## Customer-reported vegetation hazards
A customer reporting a tree branch near or touching a power line should be told this is being
logged for the vegetation management team. If the branch is currently in contact with an
energized line, the report must be escalated as a safety hazard per Safety Procedures — it is
not a routine trim request.

## Customer-owned trees
The utility trims vegetation within its clearance zone at no cost to the customer, but is not
responsible for tree health or aesthetic trimming outside that zone. Disputes are referred to
the vegetation management team, not resolved directly by customer service.
