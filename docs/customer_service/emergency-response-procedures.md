# Emergency Response Procedures

## Activation threshold
The Emergency Operations Center (EOC) activates for: outages affecting more than 500 customers,
any report of a downed line with confirmed contact (vehicle, person, structure, or water), fire
risk associated with utility equipment, or a declared Public Safety Power Shutoff (PSPS) event.

## Representative role during an activated emergency
Representatives provide the EOC's published status updates verbatim rather than improvising —
during an active emergency, outage and restoration information changes faster than usual, and
only the EOC's current bulletin should be quoted to customers.

## Life-threatening situations
Any report involving immediate danger to life — a person in contact with electrical equipment,
an active fire, or a downed line on an occupied vehicle — must be told to call 911 immediately,
in addition to the utility's own emergency dispatch. Utility crews are not a substitute for
emergency medical or fire response.

## Post-emergency
Once the EOC stands down, standard Outage Response Procedures resume for any remaining
individual outages.
