# Service Start/Stop Procedures

## Starting new service
Requires proof of address (lease or deed) and a valid ID; a deposit is required if the
applicant has no prior payment history with the utility. Standard connection is scheduled
within 1-3 business days of a completed application; same-day connection is available for an
expedited fee where technically feasible.

## Stopping service
Requires at least 1 business day's notice for a scheduled disconnect. The final bill is based on
the last actual meter read, not an estimate, whenever technically possible.

## Transferring service
A customer moving within the same service territory may transfer their account without a new
deposit if their payment history is in good standing — no more than one late payment in the
past 12 months.

## Reconnection after a nonpayment disconnect
Requires payment of the past-due balance plus a reconnection fee. Reconnection is scheduled
within 1 business day of payment confirmation under the standard cycle; same-day reconnection
is offered for medical-necessity accounts per Customer Assistance Programs.
