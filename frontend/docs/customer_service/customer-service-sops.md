# Internal Customer Service SOPs

## Call opening
Verify the account — name plus service address or customer ID — before discussing any
account-specific details, including outage or billing information, to avoid disclosing another
customer's information.

## Using Grid Copilot
Grid Copilot's answers are grounded in retrieved procedures and live system data (outage,
billing) wherever possible. Representatives remain responsible for the final response given to
the customer, and should verify anything the tool flags as low-confidence before repeating it.

## Escalation triggers
Escalate to a supervisor or the appropriate team when: a safety hazard is reported, outage
information is missing or conflicting, the customer disputes billing beyond what a standard
bill review can resolve, or the customer requests something outside a representative's standing
authority — for example, a payment plan over $500 or a compensation claim beyond the automatic
outage credit.

## Case documentation
Every customer interaction should be logged with the customer's issue, the service area or
account involved, the information given to the customer, and any escalation. This creates the
audit trail used for both quality review and the utility's own outage and billing pattern
analysis.

## Closing a case
A case is closed once the customer's question has been answered and, if applicable, an action
has been recorded — a credit request submitted, a case escalated, or information provided. An
escalated case remains open until the escalation is resolved by the receiving team.
