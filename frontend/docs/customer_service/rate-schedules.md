# Rate Schedules

## Residential Baseline
A flat per-kWh rate with no time-of-day variation. Simplest plan; best suited to customers with
steady usage throughout the day.

## Residential Tiered
The first 700 kWh per month is billed at the Tier 1 rate; usage above 700 kWh is billed at a
higher Tier 2 rate. A customer whose usage crosses the 700 kWh threshold will see a
disproportionate bill increase relative to their usage increase, because the marginal kWh above
the threshold costs more per unit.

## Time-of-Use (TOU)
Rates vary by hour: on-peak (4pm-9pm on weekdays) is priced highest, off-peak (all other hours)
lowest. Best for customers who can shift usage away from the 4pm-9pm window; can be more
expensive than Baseline for customers with high evening usage.

## Plan switching
Customers may switch rate plans once per 12-month period without a fee. A representative may
recommend a plan review if a customer's usage pattern consistently mismatches their current
plan — for example, high evening usage on a Time-of-Use plan.

## Rate changes
Published rate changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle after approval and
are never applied retroactively to a bill already issued.
