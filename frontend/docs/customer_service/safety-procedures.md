# Safety Procedures

## General principle
No representative may give an instruction that could put a customer, bystander, or field crew
member at risk of electrical shock, fire, or injury. When in doubt, the answer is always: stay
away, do not touch, and contact emergency services or the utility's emergency line.

## Reporting a safety hazard
Any report involving a downed line, sparking equipment, a burning smell near electrical
equipment, or a person in contact with electrical equipment must be treated as an emergency and
follow the Downed Power Line Procedure or Emergency Response Procedures immediately — do not
attempt normal outage troubleshooting first.

## Standard safety guidance representatives may give
- Never touch, approach, or drive over a downed power line. Treat every downed line as
  energized, even if it appears dead or is lying still on the ground.
- Keep people, pets, and vehicles at least 30 feet away from a downed line or damaged equipment.
- If a downed line is touching a vehicle, occupants should stay inside unless there is an
  immediate life-threatening hazard such as fire — in that case, exit by jumping clear with both
  feet together and shuffle away without touching the vehicle and ground at the same time.

## What representatives must never do
Never instruct a customer to move, cut, reset, or otherwise interact with a downed line,
damaged equipment, or a breaker or meter that shows visible damage. Never estimate whether a
line is "probably safe" — every downed line is treated as live until a field crew confirms
otherwise.
